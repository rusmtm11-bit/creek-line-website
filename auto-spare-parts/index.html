Overview

Creek Line Limited sources OEM and aftermarket parts for passenger, commercial, and heavy-duty vehicles. Each part is verified against the applicable specification — dimensions, materials, and markings — prior to shipment. This standard applies equally to individual component orders and bulk distribution orders.

Additional Information

  • Parts are checked against technical drawings or reference samples where these are provided by the client.
  • Order volumes range from individual components to full container shipments, with the same verification process applied in each case.
